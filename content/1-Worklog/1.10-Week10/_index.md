* Learn the concepts of cloud-based **Data Governance** (access management, data security, and data quality).
* Explore and practice with **Amazon QuickSight** to connect data, build visual dashboards, and create analytical reports.

### Week 10 Achievements:

* **Mastered Data Governance Concepts:**
  * Understood access control mechanisms based on Least Privilege principles.
  * Learned how to encrypt data at rest and in transit using AWS KMS.
  * Grasped data quality standards and behavioral monitoring via AWS audit logs (CloudTrail).

* **Mastered Amazon QuickSight:**
  * Successfully connected structured data sources from databases to QuickSight.
  * Created Analyses and designed various interactive visual charts (Pie charts, Bar charts, Line graphs) representing invoice statistics over time, by customers, and by total amounts.
  * Formulated calculated fields to compute automated aggregate metrics (Sum, Average).
  * Successfully published and shared complete dashboards for business intelligence, decision-making, and report sharing.
